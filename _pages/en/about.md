<div class="ey-section" style="margin-top:0.5rem">
<p>
I research <b>lightweight computer vision models</b> that run in real time on edge devices.
To make deep learning work within real hardware constraints, I handle the whole pipeline myself —
from model design to <b>compression, quantization (FP16/INT8), and NPU deployment</b>.
</p>
<p>
I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Sangmyung University, where I worked in the Computer Vision Lab (CCLAB)
on on-device AI projects across healthcare, security, and mobility: organoid vasculature classification,
NPU-based iris recognition, and in-vehicle face and emotion recognition. I have hands-on experience optimizing models
for FuriosaAI Warboy, Mythic, and Kneron NPUs as well as NVIDIA Jetson.
</p>
</div>
