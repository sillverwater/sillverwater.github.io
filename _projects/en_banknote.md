<div class="ey-section"><h2>Background</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Lack of datasets for Russian ruble banknote serial number recognition</li>
  <li>Need for an OCR model that accurately recognizes serial numbers including Cyrillic characters</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="ey-section"><h2>Process</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Labeled 12,998 serial number images from 7 Russian ruble denominations</li>
  <li>Applied Gaussian noise augmentation and built the training dataset</li>
  <li>Designed a CRNN combining ResNet-based feature extraction with BiLSTM-based sequence prediction</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="ey-section"><h2>Results</h2>
<ul>
  <li>99% overall serial number recognition accuracy</li>
  <li>Built a Russian ruble serial number dataset including Cyrillic characters and proposed an automatic recognition model</li>
  <li>Poster Session presentation at a domestic conference (CEIC 2023)</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="ey-section"><h2>Contribution</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Led the entire study - dataset construction, model development, performance validation</li>
  <li>Paper writing and conference presentation</li>
</ul>
</div>
